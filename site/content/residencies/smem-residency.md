In 2026, Kimina and [Goffbaby](https://goffbaby.com) came together for a three-week residency at SMEM—the Swiss Museum and Centre for Electronic Music Instruments in Fribourg. The residency was developed as part of a Pro Helvetia Synergies collaboration.

SMEM’s collection and Playroom created the setting for an extended collaborative process around electronic sound and music-making. The residency concludes with a public recap of the process and a live set on 5 September 2026.
