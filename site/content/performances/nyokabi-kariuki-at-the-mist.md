Experimental sound artist Nyokabi Kariũki (@nkariuki_)puts together a farewell show to give thanks to her musical community in Nairobi before she leaves the country to pursue further studies in the world of music composition 🌟

Supported by experimental live and DJ sets from Kimina (@kiminaofficial), naisiS (@__sisian), Ng'at Maler (@ngatmaler), and Coco Em (@cocoem__), Nyokabi will perform music from her released records 'peace places: kenyan memories', 'FEELING BODY' as well as some unreleased gems. Come to say hello and goodbye at the MIST, The Mall, Westlands from 7pm till late 🤍
