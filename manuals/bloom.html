Bloom

BLOOM listens for the dominant pitch of what you play and grows sympathetic resonance around it: four stereo string-like layers tuned to the fundamental, octave, twelfth and double octave.

It retunes gently as the music moves, holds its tuning when you stop, and keeps feedback just below runaway. Eternal pushes the layers toward endless sustain and slowly trades energy between them.

Working with it

  1. BLOOM tracks one pitch at a time, roughly 45 Hz to 1.6 kHz; monophonic lines and vocals work best.
  2. On chords it latches onto the strongest pitch, which can be a beautiful, unexpected drone.
  3. The header shows the detected note; the curves swell with each layer's energy.
  4. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  5. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  6. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  7. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  8. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
