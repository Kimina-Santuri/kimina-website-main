Dust

DUST lifts the fine detail and transients out of recent audio and scatters them as a stereo granular cloud. It records continuously and starts sounding once it holds 1.5 seconds; until then you hear the dry signal.

Four macros cover everything. Extract both isolates detail and acts as the wet amount, so turning it up is turning the effect up.

Controls

Auto GainHolds the level steady as you change settings, so you hear the effect, not the volume.Off
ExtractIsolates high detail and sets the wet amount: up is more dust, less source.65 %
DensityGrain length and activity: long sparse flecks to a thick, fast cloud.45 %
ScatterHow far back grains reach (up to ~6 s) and how much their speed varies.35 %
SpaceStereo spread of grains and the size of their cross-coupled tail.35 %

Presets

Working with it

  1. DUST works best on material with detail: drums, percussion, breathy vocals, field recordings.
  2. Because Extract is also the wet amount, automate it alone to fade the effect in and out.
  3. After reloading or a reset it needs 1.5 seconds of audio before the cloud appears.
  4. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  5. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  6. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  7. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  8. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
