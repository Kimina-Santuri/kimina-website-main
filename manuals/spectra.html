Spectra

SPECTRA works on the spectrum instead of the waveform. It splits the sound into 2049 frequency bands about 47 times a second, reshapes their levels, and puts the sound back together. With every control at rest it passes audio through unchanged, one 85 ms frame late (reported to the host, so it stays in time).

Freeze holds the current spectrum as an endless, gently moving drone. Blur lets sound linger in time, Smear spreads it across neighbouring frequencies, and Shift moves the whole spectrum up or down in pitch.

Working with it

  1. Freeze is a parameter: automate it on the last beat of a phrase, or map it to a footswitch.
  2. Shift without Freeze is a smooth, slightly phasey pitch shifter that keeps the timing of the input.
  3. Thin plus Blur turns drums and noise into pitched, glassy tones.
  4. SPECTRA reports 85 ms of latency; the host compensates, so keep plugin delay compensation on.
  5. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  6. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  7. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  8. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  9. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
