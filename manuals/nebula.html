K.SPACE.ECHO.SHIMMER

Kimina · manual

Nebula

NEBULA is a vast modulated space, from rhythmic tempo-locked echoes to endless clouds. An eight-line feedback network sets its line lengths from the song's tempo; Warp morphs those lengths from nearly equal (echoes) to scattered (a dense cloud).

Shimmer pitch-shifts part of the tail inside the loop by an interval you choose, so it climbs (or falls) as it decays. Decay runs up to a minute and Freeze holds it forever.

Working with it

  1. Shimmer and Hi Cut work together: a lower Hi Cut stops repeated octaves from turning into fizz.
  2. Warp at 0 with Density at 0 is a clean tempo delay; raise Density alone for a diffused delay.
  3. Spacing changes glide like tape, so automating it gives pitch swoops rather than clicks.
  4. Freeze, then play over the top: new sound passes dry while the frozen space holds.
  5. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click to reset it.
  6. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  7. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  8. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  9. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
