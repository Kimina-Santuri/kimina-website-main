K.CUT.SHUFFLE.PLAY

Kimina · manual

Kata

KATA (Swahili for "cut") chops a loop into slices and plays them back as a new pattern in time with your song. Drop a loop on it, press Shuffle until something hits, lock the steps you like, and shuffle the rest. Every slice is also on a key, so you can play them yourself.

Slices are cut at the hits, at the quiet gaps, on a beat grid or into equal parts. The pattern maker listens to each slice (how much low end, how bright, how loud) and puts low hits on the downbeats, bright ones on the ticks and loud mid ones on the backbeats, using rhythm families from four-on-the-floor and breaks to afro house, amapiano and club. Each step can repeat (ratchet), change pitch, play backwards or fade out. Swing, Skip, Drift, Wobble and Tight change how the pattern feels while it plays.

Drop a video (MP4 or MOV, up to two minutes) instead of a loop and KATA slices its sound; the picture next to the waveform follows whatever slice plays, so repeats, pitch and reverse all show on screen. The sound is saved in your set and the video is linked by its location: if you move the file, the sound still plays and KATA asks you to find it.

Float opens the video in its own window (resize it, keep it on top, or double-click for full screen on a projector); it keeps following the slices with the plugin window closed. Bounce renders the pattern for 1 to 16 bars to Music/KATA Bounces: a WAV to drag into Live, and for a video an MP4 with the picture re-cut to match. Each bounce is a new take (Skip, Drift and Wobble roll again).

The loop is saved inside your set. The pattern runs while Live plays (or, with Play set to Hold, while you hold a key below Base).

Working with it

  1. Click a star in the constellation or a slice on the waveform to hear it; drag a star onto a step to put it there.
  2. Right-click a step to lock it; Shuffle keeps locked steps and rolls the rest.
  3. Drag a chop line on the waveform to move it (it snaps to hits; hold Shift to move freely). Alt-click adds a chop, Alt-click a line removes it. Reset goes back to automatic slicing.
  4. Scroll over the waveform to zoom in on the chops; scroll sideways to move along.
  5. Click the video to make it bigger; B/W shows it in black and white.
  6. Back swaps to the pattern before the last Shuffle, and pressing it again swaps forward.
  7. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  8. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  9. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  10. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  11. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
