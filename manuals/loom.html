Loom

LOOM is a performance granular time-stretcher. It records the last few seconds continuously and replays them through four overlapping grains at up to ten thousand times slower, with pitch, scatter and reverse independent of speed.

Freeze captures a loop to sculpt indefinitely, Auto Freeze captures one when the input crosses a threshold, and the Space page sends the grains into a large reverb with octave shimmer. It passes dry audio until three seconds are recorded.

Working with it

  1. Four pages: Core for the main sound, Grains for detail, Perform for freezing and sync, Space for the reverb.
  2. Freeze, then sweep Position to scrub through the captured loop; Retrigger restarts it.
  3. Sync ties grain length to the host tempo; Division sets it in beats.
  4. At very high Stretch the sound barely moves: use Scatter and Drift to keep it alive.
  5. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click to reset it.
  6. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  7. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  8. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  9. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
