Tide

TIDE is an ambient delay whose read heads move. Time picks a tempo-locked delay from an eighth of a beat to 64 beats, and at the top of its range feedback becomes near-infinite.

Current sets the heads drifting: positive values stretch the delay until playback slows and reverses, negative values shorten it so repeats speed up and rise in pitch. Two overlapping heads keep every reset silent.

Working with it

  1. Time moves in beat steps; the header shows the current length in beats and seconds.
  2. Small Current values (±10–20 %) give tape-like drift; large ones turn repeats into reverse swells.
  3. At the top of Time, Feedback still controls how fast the near-infinite loop decays.
  4. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  5. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  6. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  7. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  8. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
