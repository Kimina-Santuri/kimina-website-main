Fracture

FRACTURE breaks a signal on the grid. At every slice boundary of the host's transport it rolls the dice: pass the audio through, or replace it with a captured fragment that may repeat, skip ahead or play backwards.

Chaos adds sample-rate reduction and bit-crushing to the fragments, and Decay leaves a short cross-stereo echo of each one. It needs half a second of audio before it starts breaking anything.

Working with it

  1. FRACTURE follows the host's beat position while playing and free-runs at the host tempo while stopped.
  2. Automate Fracture from 0 to 100 % over the last bar of a section for an instant fill.
  3. On a drum bus keep Chaos low for rhythmic edits; raise it for destruction.
  4. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click (Ctrl-click on Windows) to reset it.
  5. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  6. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  7. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  8. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
