Halo

HALO is a stereo reverb whose decay is set by the song, not a knob. Choose one bar, half a bar or one beat and the tail fades by 60 dB in exactly that time at the current tempo, following tempo changes and automation.

Two engines share the front end: Hall, a dense eight-line feedback network, and Plate, Jon Dattorro's classic figure-of-eight plate. They crossfade without clicks, so Character can be automated.

Working with it

  1. On a return track set Mix to 100 % and blend with the send knob; on an insert keep Mix around 20–40 %.
  2. Automate Sync from 1/4 to 1/1 into a drop for a tail that opens with the song.
  3. Duck plus a long Sync gives a big space that never smears fast parts.
  4. Freeze is a parameter: automate it on the last beat of a phrase to hold a chord through a transition.
  5. Drag a slider to set it, hold Shift while dragging for fine control, scroll over it to nudge, and double-click or Cmd-click to reset it.
  6. Hover any control to read what it does in the footer.
  7. ‹ › step through the factory presets; click the preset name for the full list. A * means you have edited it.
  8. + saves the current settings as a user preset: type a name and press Enter (or + again). Saving under a used name replaces it. User presets appear under the factory ones; × deletes one, and Folder opens where they are kept, to back up or share them.
  9. A / B holds two versions of your settings: tweak one, click the other letter to compare, click back to return.
